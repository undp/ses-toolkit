About us

The Social and Environmental Standards (SES) reinforce UNDP’s commitment to mainstream social and environmental sustainability in our Programmes and Projects to support sustainable development. The standards are integral to UNDP’s programming quality assurance and risk management approach and assist UNDP staff, implementing partners, and responsible parties in managing social and environmental risks and impacts.

Our team

The UNDP Social and Environmental Standards Policy Team sits within UNDP’s Effectiveness Group in the Bureau for Policy and Programme Support (BPPS). The Effectiveness Group provides programme-related corporate policy services and establishes the standards, policies, instruments, systems, and processes used to deliver high development impact and results across the organization.

Programming Principles

The SES reinforce a "principled" approach to all UNDP programming. UNDP applies the UN Sustainable Development Group guiding principles for UN Sustainable Development Cooperation Frameworks. These principles form the normative foundation for integrated programming in all country contexts, with "leave no one behind" as the overarching and unifying principle.

Project-Level Standards

At the project level, UNDP Standards 1-8 further support the implementation of UNDP's commitments to promoting that no one is left behind; human rights; gender equality and women's empowerment; sustainability and resilience; and accountability (collectively, the SES Programming Principles). The Project-Level Standards set out specific requirements for different social and environmental issues.

UNDP Social and Environmental Standards

In alignment with UNDP's Strategic Plan, the UNDP Social and Environmental Standards (SES) reinforce the organization's commitment to integrating social and environmental sustainability in our programmes and projects to support sustainable development. The SES are UNDP policy and an integral component of UNDP's quality assurance and risk management approach, assisting UNDP staff, implementing partners, and responsible parties to manage social and environmental risks and impacts of UNDP programmes and projects.

UNDP SESP Online Tool

UNDP’s Social and Environmental Screening Procedure (SESP) is a critical risk management tool that guides users through the risk screening and categorization process, resulting in a project-level risk category (Low, Moderate, Substantial, or High) that reflects the level of review, assessment, and resources required for addressing and managing a project’s potential social and environmental risks and adverse impacts.

SES Policy Update

UNDP’s Social and Environmental Standards (SES) were approved by UNDP’s Organizational Performance Group (OPG) in June 2014 and went into effect in January 2015. The SES have been reviewed and updated, with the revised SES effective as of January 1, 2021. To support a transition to the updated SES, a transition period and implementation plan have been introduced.
